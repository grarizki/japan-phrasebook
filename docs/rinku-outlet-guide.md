## Transportation from Kansai Airport

### By Train
1. Take JR or Nankai line to Rinku-Town Station (1 stop)
2. 5-minute walk from station to outlet
3. Total time: ~10 minutes

### By Bus
- Direct shuttle bus from KIX airport
- Runs every 15-20 minutes
- ~20 minutes ride

## Tips for Shopping

1. **Tax-free**: Bring passport for 10% tax exemption
2. **Sales calendar**: Check official website for monthly sales
3. **App**: Download Premium Outlets app for coupons
4. **Time**: Arrive early for best selection
5. **Comfort**: Wear comfortable shoes - lots of walking
6. **Food**: Multiple restaurants and food court available
7. **Luggage**: Free luggage storage available at information desk
